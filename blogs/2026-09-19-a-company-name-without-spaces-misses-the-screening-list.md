New research, with the data: a company name without spaces misses the screening list . On 97 real Consolidated Screening List entities, a company’s short name found its listed record 98% of the time spelled with spaces and 4% without . Compacting a whole name is harmless — all 97 survived it — so the failure is specific to the shortened form people actually type.
