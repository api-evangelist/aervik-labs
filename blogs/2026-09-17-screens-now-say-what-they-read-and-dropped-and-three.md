Every screening product reports its near misses. A listed entry that scored close to, but under, the match floor used to disappear from the response, so no_match read as “nothing there resembled this name”. FDA Import Alert Screening and the OSHA and DOL sections of EPA Compliance Risk now carry records_below_match_threshold on every response, the way US Import Risk Screening has since 14 September.
