# Aervik Labs

> Narrow, well-documented compliance and trade-data APIs. Each one does a single job with a
> documented request and response, and is billed through the RapidAPI marketplace.

Aervik Labs is operated by EAC Ventures, LLC. It is AI-operated and human-directed: pricing,
publication standards, corrections and spend are decided or approved by a human principal.
Full detail at https://aerviklabs.com/about/.

Every figure published on this site cites a public source or is our own measurement,
published with its method and date. We do not publish
testimonials, review scores, user counts, or case studies, because we do not have any yet.

## APIs

- [ACORD 25 Data Extraction & COI Verification](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/coi-verification/): Verify a certificate against your requirements — and get told plainly when a value could not be read.
- [EPA ECHO Facility Violation History & Risk Score](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/epa-compliance-risk/): Look up a U.S. facility's environmental compliance history — EPA's alleged violations and formal enforcement — and get one risk score with a plain-English summary.
- [FDA Import Alert Screening](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/fda-import-alerts/): Screen a firm against every FDA Import Alert Red, Yellow and Green List in one call.
- [Landed Cost API — HS Subheading & Duty Range](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/hts-tariff-classifier/): Estimate what a product costs to import: 6-digit HS subheading, duty rate range, and China Section 301 list exposure from a plain-English description.
- [UFLPA Entity List Screening](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/import-risk-screening/): UFLPA Entity List and denied-party screening in one call, from two live federal sources.
- [Invoice & Receipt OCR](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/invoice-ocr/): Turn a photo, scan, or PDF of an invoice or receipt into structured JSON: vendor, line items, tax, and total.
- [PII Redaction](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/pii-redaction/): Find and redact names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, SSNs, card numbers and dates of birth in free text.
- [Resume Parser](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/resume-parser/): Turn a resume — plain text, a PDF, or an image — into structured candidate data.
- [SEC Form 4 Insider Trade Alerts](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/sec-form4-alerts/): A public company's recent Form 4 insider buy/sell filings as structured data, live from SEC EDGAR, with plain-English context per transaction.
- [SAM.gov Exclusions & OFAC Screening](https://aerviklabs.com/apis/vendor-entity-screening/): Screen a vendor company against SAM.gov Exclusions and the OFAC/BIS Consolidated Screening List in one call — the check a procurement team runs before onboarding a new vendor. (not yet open for signups)
